Android records still without a real-device observation, 2026-11-24

The audit below compares each Android record's c hash with every c hash a
real device produced in this work: the 33-build cloud-phone grid, the phone
captures, the TLS 1.2 capping runs and the WebView service reports. 23 of
27 base records are backed by a real device - 21 match a captured c hash,
and android_webview125 and android_webview139 were captured (and one
server-classified) on real hardware already. Four have no such backing:

android_okhttp7   t12d1208h2_d34a8e72043a_31a7e1922da8
  OkHttp on Android 7: TLS 1.2, 8 extensions, no session_ticket, no
  status_request, and signature algorithms without the rsa_pss family.
  No Android 7 runtime can be booted here (no redroid image for it; the
  OnePlus 9R is Android 13). The record stays a peet capture with
  fidelity "close" because BoringSSL cannot send two of its signature
  algorithms either.

android_okhttp8   t12d1209h2_d34a8e72043a_e08eabe7240f
  Its cipher list hashes to d34a8e72043a, and a real OkHttp 3.12 capped to
  TLS 1.2 on the cloud phone produced exactly that (raw
  android_okhttp_tls12_android13.json). The extension set is not yet
  reproduced: the capped capture is missing session_ticket and ALPN,
  because replacing the socket factory drops the extensions the library
  set on it. So the ciphers are confirmed on hardware and the extension
  list is not.

probe limit found while doing this
  OkHttp 3.12 in the probe never sends an ALPN extension, though OkHttp 4.12
  does with the same builder calls. The captured android_okhttp3_12 record
  therefore has no ALPN either, and its a-segment reads t13d151100 rather
  than t13d1511h1. That is a limit of this capture path, not a claim about
  OkHttp: a real 3.12 on Android would offer h2 and http/1.1. It is listed
  here rather than hidden, and the record's own source says the same.

What is verified instead, and how
  21 base records match a c hash a real device produced. WebView 125's
  padded shape (b1ff8ab2d16f) was reported by tls.peet.ws itself for a
  cloud-phone connection; WebView 139 came from the Android 10 container;
  every Conscrypt, Cronet and OkHttp-3.12/4.x/5.x record came from an APK
  that links that exact build, running on Android 8.1 through 14.

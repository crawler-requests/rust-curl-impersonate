behind it. "byte-exact" means a real Android runtime sent a ClientHello
whose JA4 equals the record's; "composed" means every element of the hello
is byte-exact from a device, and the record is that hello plus or minus one
extension whose effect on the hash is computed from the verified hello;
"unreproduced" means no device here can produce the shape.

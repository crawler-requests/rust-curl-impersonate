cronet_android100
  The JA4 differs only in its last segment, and that is the reason the record
  is `close`: Cronet greases its cipher, group and extension lists, while
  BoringSSL's GREASE switch greases five lists together, so a GREASE value
  lands in a different position on each connection. The extension set, the
  cipher list minus GREASE, and the HTTP/2 fingerprint all match exactly.

swiftnio_ssl
  Full match, including the HTTP/2 SETTINGS a BoringSSL-based Swift client
  sends (`3:100;6:16384`) rather than a browser's.

tlsclient_safari27_2
  Full match. This is macOS Safari 27.2, captured with tls-client's master
  profile; iOS 27 still has no capture.
